Cart Check

The app can't read DCBS's live cart itself (session-routing — see docs/ORDERING-PROCESS.md), so paste it here by hand before you submit: copy everything out of the cart or the "Create Order from Pull List" page and drop it in below, one item per line, however messy — price and quantity text along with the title is fine. Each line gets checked against every order you've already synced, so anything you've bought before — a different cycle's pull-list match, a title the app already stuck onto your sticky list after a direct purchase — gets flagged before it becomes a real duplicate on a placed order.
