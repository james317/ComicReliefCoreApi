Cart Check

The app can't read DCBS's live cart itself (session-routing — see docs/ORDERING-PROCESS.md), so hand it the cart before you submit. Every item gets checked against every order you've already synced, so anything you've bought before gets flagged before it becomes a real duplicate on a placed order.

Upload a cart PDF

On DCBS's cart page in Safari, take a screenshot, choose Full Page, and save it as a PDF. Uploading it also checks each item's quantity.

Or paste the cart

One item per line, however messy — price and quantity text along with the title is fine.
