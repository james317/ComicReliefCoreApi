Shipments

The steps for a box that just arrived: pick the shipment below (pulled straight from DCBS — no packing-list upload needed), upload that shipment's CLZ export to see each issue's real release date, then read in ship-date order. Once you've scanned everything in, sync your order history to flag anything on your pull list that should have shown up in this shipment but didn't.

    Release dates for this shipment's issues

    Do a CLZ export of just this shipment's issues (not your whole collection) and upload it here — this only adds/updates those issues' release dates and never touches your full collection snapshot on the Pull List page.

    Fix a missing or wrong ship date by hand

    For the odd item CLZ never has a clean date for (no catalog record, or a reprint showing its original printing's date). Series should read as the start of the item's own title, up to the issue number — e.g. "Devils Due Presents Lovebunny & Mr Hell" for a line titled "Devils Due Presents Lovebunny & Mr Hell #1 (One Shot) Cvr B…" — not necessarily how CLZ itself lists the series, since this has to match the shipment line's own title text. Leave the date blank to clear one back out.
